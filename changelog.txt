= 0.3.10 - 2026-10-08 =
* Fixed - Galleries, sliders and variation scripts that change the src or srcset of a wrapped image no longer keep showing the original photo. A small inline footer script parks the picture's WebP/AVIF source srcsets while another image is shown and restores them when the original returns. Lazy-loading placeholders, foreign pictures and unmarked images are left untouched.
* Compatibility - The inline script is excluded from WP Rocket delayed JavaScript, Cloudflare Rocket Loader and common minifiers so it runs before visitors interact with galleries.
